PRE-REGISTRATION -- trader-practice lens, RSI divergence on BTC/USD 4h (written 2026-10-14 before any run)

Question: is the study fair to how divergence traders actually trade? I test commonly taught
RSI-divergence trading rules on the same data, costs, periods and selection protocol.

DATA / PERIODS / COSTS (same as the study)
  data/BTCUSD_4h.csv from 2013-01-01; RSI(14) Pine-compatible; ATR(14) as pinetest.smc.atr; EMA200.
  PRE 2014-01-01..2017-12-31, TRAIN 2018-01-01..2021-12-31, TEST 2022-01-01..end. A trade belongs
  to the period of its signal bar. Costs 0.1% per side (primary) and 0.05% (secondary), charged on
  entry + exit notional, in R (multiples of initial risk). One trade at a time.

GRID (3 x 2 x 5 x 2 x 11 = 660 configs)
  detector (3):
    rsi_pivstop  : TV-style pivots on RSI (left=right=lb), price compared at the RSI pivot bars,
                   previous pivot 5..60 bars earlier, signal on pivot+right. Stop beyond the price
                   extreme of the RSI-pivot bar (the study's rule).
    rsi_swingstop: same signals, stop beyond the actual swing extreme = lowest low (long) / highest
                   high (short) over [pivot-lb, signal bar].
    price_pivot  : pivots on price (low for bullish, high for bearish; strict, left=right=lb);
                   compare RSI at those two price-pivot bars (prev 5..60 bars earlier). Signal on
                   pivot+right. Stop beyond the price pivot (equals the swing extreme).
    All stops get the study's 0.1 ATR(signal bar) buffer.
  lb (2): 3, 5
  setup (5):
    regbull     : regular bullish (price LL, RSI HL), long only, no filter
    regbull_os  : regular bullish, long only, RSI at the FIRST (earlier) pivot < 30
    regular     : regular bullish long + regular bearish short, no filter
    regular_ext : regular, bullish needs first-pivot RSI < 30, bearish first-pivot RSI > 70
    hidden_trend: hidden bullish long if close > EMA200, hidden bearish short if close < EMA200
  entry (2):
    next_open   : market at the open after the signal bar (study)
    confirm     : wait for a close beyond the pivot bar's high (long) / low (short), checked from
                  the signal bar itself up to 10 bars after it; enter at the next open. Cancelled
                  if the stop trades first or no confirmation within 10 bars.
  exit (11), all with the structural stop live from the fill bar (gap at open fills at the open;
  stop beats any same-bar target):
    rr1, rr2, rr3 : fixed target rr x risk, time stop at the close of bar 30 (study)
    rsi50         : exit at the next open after the first close (fill bar on) with RSI >= 50
                    (long) / <= 50 (short); cap 60 bars
    rsi7030       : same with 70 (long) / 30 (short); cap 60 bars
    oppdiv        : exit at the next open after an opposite regular divergence (same detector and
                    lb, no filter) is confirmed on or after the fill bar; cap 60 bars
    chand2, chand3: chandelier trailing stop max(initial stop, highest high since fill - k*ATR)
                    (mirrored for shorts), updated on each close, used from the next bar; cap 60
    time12, time30, time60: exit at the close of the N-th bar (fill bar = bar 1)

SELECTION (on TRAIN at 0.1% cost, n >= 30): (a) highest average R, (b) highest trade Sharpe
(mean R / std R). Both picks judged on TEST and PRE at 0.1% and 0.05%. Also repeat the pick at
0.05% cost.

PLACEBOS for each pick: 300 reps per period; random signal bars drawn uniformly between the first
and last real signal bar of the period, same count, same side as each real trade, same risk % of
entry, same exit rule (rr target / RSI level / next opposite divergence / chandelier / time),
market entry at next open, one at a time. Report % of placebo avg R below the real avg R.

GRID REPORTS: TEST median avg R; number of configs positive in TEST; number positive in all three
periods (n=0 counts as not positive); same at 0.05% cost; marginal TEST medians per dimension
value; Spearman rank correlation TRAIN vs TEST. Benchmark for "all three positive": the same count
in a placebo version of the grid (each config's trades moved to random bars, same side/risk/exit).

DECISION RULE (fixed now)
  The study is called materially unfair to divergence traders if EITHER
   (1) a TRAIN-chosen trader rule (pick a or b) has TEST avg R > 0 at 0.1% cost AND beats >= 90%
       of random-bars-same-side placebos on TEST AND PRE avg R > 0; OR
   (2) the trader-rule grid's TEST median avg R > 0, or its count of configs positive in all three
       periods clearly exceeds the placebo-grid count (above the placebo 95th percentile).
  Otherwise the negative result stands (trader rules do not rescue it), and I will quantify how
  much each dimension moves the result.
